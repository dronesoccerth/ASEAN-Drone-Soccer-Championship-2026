Drone Soccer Class 20 — 3×3 (Brushed)

20 cm Drone Soccer • 3 players • Brushed Motor • OPEN

Open original PDF ↗
Website content is summarized and structured from the latest supplied rulebooks. The original PDF remains the primary reference document.
Players3 players
AgeOpen age
Drone20±1 cm
Weight≤110 g
Field≈3×6 m
Goal / Time≈30–37 cm
01 · Participant Eligibility
  1. The team has 3 members, with all 3 competing in each match.
  2. No age limit.
  3. One team controller/coach may be present (or none), but may not participate in the match.
  4. A participant may not be listed on another team within the same rule category.
02 · Drone Requirements
  1. Any brand of spherical four-propeller drone is permitted; diameter 20cm±1cm; maximum weight 110g including battery, excluding transmitter.
  2. Motor requirement: Brushed only.
  3. The drone must be remotely controlled by a transmitter.
  4. Battery information must be clearly labelled. Missing or illegible labels are not allowed.
  5. The drone must be presented to officials for inspection before every entry to the competition field.
03 · Competition Rules
  1. Indoor match on an approximately 3×6 m field. Two teams compete, designated Red and Blue. Player positions must be declared so the Striker can be tagged.
  2. Each set is 3 minutes, best-of-three sets (first to 2 sets). For Class 20, a set may be reduced to 2 minutes when entries are numerous; for Class 40, time may be adjusted as appropriate.
  3. Take-off is permitted only after the referee's start signal.
  4. Only the Striker may score, by flying through the opponent's goal ring.
  5. After a Striker scores, the goal LED lights. All players must retreat behind the Center Line before the score is counted. A new scoring attempt begins after the LED turns off; a goal made before that signal is not counted.
  6. If any player can no longer continue flying, they must put down the transmitter and raise both hands to notify the referee.
  7. At the end of time, competitors must land and stop the propellers. No one may enter the field until officials allow drone retrieval.
  8. The team with the higher number of valid ring-through goals wins.
  9. A team that intentionally interferes with the opponent's flight and causes a flight accident may be disqualified.
  10. All on-field rulings, decisions and referee discretion are final.
04 · Competition Field
  1. Field dimensions are approximately 3×6 m, enclosed by safety netting.
  2. Goal ring diameter is approximately 30–37 cm.
  3. A score is awarded only when the drone passes completely through the Goal.
  4. Positions: STRIKER scores; SWEEPER supports attack/defence and cannot score; KEEPER protects the goal and cannot score.
  5. Red and Blue players stand outside the net on their respective sides and attempt to fly through the opponent's goal.
05 · Responsibility & Notes
  1. Each team is responsible for the safety of its own drone and for accidents caused by its members or equipment.
  2. The organizer is not responsible for incidents and/or accidents arising from participation or competitors' equipment.
  3. Any rule changes will be announced on competition day, and officials will brief the rules again before the event starts.

Super Pilot — Junior

Individual pilot challenge • Age 12 or under

Open original PDF ↗
Website content is summarized and structured from the latest supplied rulebooks. The original PDF remains the primary reference document.
Players1 person
Age≤12
DroneSkykick 2 / EVO
Weight≤120 g
Field≈4×8 m
Goal / Time3 min
01 · Participant Eligibility
  1. One competitor.
  2. Competitor age: 12 or under. Age is calculated by birth year against competition year B.E. 2569, without month/day; the source states the birth-year criterion as: born in B.E. 2557 (2014) or later.
  3. One team controller/coach may be present (or none).
02 · Drone Requirements
  1. Only Skykick 2 / Skykick EVO is allowed; 20 cm diameter, maximum 120 g including battery and excluding transmitter.
  2. Remote control by transmitter is required.
  3. Only the correct Skykick battery model is allowed; modification is prohibited.
  4. Battery information label must be clearly readable; missing/illegible labels are not permitted.
  5. The drone must be presented for inspection before every field entry.
03 · Competition Rules
  1. Before the run, submit the drone for inspection.
  2. After the start signal, the competitor has 3 minutes to follow the obstacle flight guideline, circle the poles and complete 10 ring goals, then stop the time.
  3. The drone may touch obstacles but must not damage them.
  4. At 3 minutes, the competitor must land.
  5. Two runs are given. The best result—completion of the full course in the shortest time—is used. An exact tie requires a re-run.
  6. No entry into the field until time ends and officials permit drone retrieval.
  7. Interference with another competitor causing a flight accident results in disqualification.
  8. All rulings and referee discretion are final.
04 · Competition Field
  1. Indoor field approximately 4×8 m with safety netting.
  2. Ring goal diameter approximately 30–37 cm.
  3. Obstacle pole height approximately 2 m.
  4. The original rulebook provides the flight guideline and top-view layout.
05 · Responsibility & Notes
  1. Teams are responsible for their drone safety and accidents caused by their members or drones.
  2. The organizer is not responsible for incidents/accidents arising from participation or equipment.
  3. Rule changes, if any, will be announced on competition day.

Drone Mission — Junior

Drone mission challenge • Age 12 or under

Open original PDF ↗
Website content is summarized and structured from the latest supplied rulebooks. The original PDF remains the primary reference document.
Players1 person
Age≤12
DroneSoccer drone
Weight≤120 g
Field≈4×8 m
Goal / Time3 min × 2
01 · Participant Eligibility
  1. One competitor.
  2. Competitor age: 12 or under. Age is calculated by birth year against competition year B.E. 2569, without month/day; the source states the birth-year criterion as: born in B.E. 2557 (2014) or later.
  3. One team controller/coach may be present (or none).
02 · Drone Requirements
  1. Any size/brand/model of soccer drone is allowed, maximum 120 g including battery and excluding transmitter.
  2. The drone must be remotely controlled by a transmitter.
  3. Any battery type may be used, but voltage/current information must be clearly labelled. Degraded, illegibly labelled, leaking or swollen batteries are strictly prohibited.
  4. A spare drone is allowed if presented to officials for advance inspection.
03 · Competition Rules
  1. Indoor netted field approximately 4×8 m. One team competes at a time, with 2 attempts of 3 minutes each; the best attempt is used for ranking.
  2. Within each 3-minute attempt, the competitor may take off as many times as needed.
  3. Start by placing the drone at the 200×60 cm Take-off area, then leave the field and wait for the referee's signal.
  4. After take-off, the pilot must remain outside the field boundary and may walk/run outside it. If any part of the body enters the field, officials will stop the attempt.
  5. Fly the prescribed route continuously for 5 laps and land in the Landing area within 3 minutes. If any part of the drone extends outside the Landing area, 20 seconds are added.
  6. If the drone falls, crashes, fails, or touches the net/ring/pole etc., lap counting must restart from Take-off while the clock continues.
  7. Battery changes are permitted during the attempt while time continues.
  8. Two obstacle rings, approximately 80–90 cm in diameter, are placed on the route.
  9. The team controller may not enter the competition area or assist the competitor during the run.
  10. Interference causing a flight accident may lead to disqualification; referee rulings and discretion are final.
04 · Competition Field
  1. Field approximately 4×8 m with surrounding safety net.
  2. Two pole locations and a figure-eight flight path are specified in the source diagram.
  3. Two obstacle rings approximately 80 cm in diameter.
05 · Responsibility & Notes
  1. Teams are responsible for drone safety and accidents caused by their members or drones.
  2. The organizer is not responsible for incidents/accidents arising from participation or equipment.
  3. Rule changes, if any, will be announced on competition day.
